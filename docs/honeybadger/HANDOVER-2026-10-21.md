## Decisions made where the author's sheet was silent (check with the author)

- Burrow needs Tier 1 (as on H0's tier list) and covers only the six Organic army kinds.
- Burrowed units can't fire and don't collide (SC2 behaviour). They are still targetable, because nothing is hidden in this game.
- The drone is cast at a point and stays leashed within 220 of it. It heals units before buildings. It can be shot (60 HP).
- In-combat shield regeneration is at the full rate (2/s, 6/s in the field), with no reduced in-combat rate.
